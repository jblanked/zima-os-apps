# Cooler Control
Manage your CPU and GPU temperatures and performance.

The Docker image is from https://docs.coolercontrol.org/installation/docker.html

## Installation
1. Open up your ZimaOS Dashboard.
2. Open up the `App Store`
3. Click on `My Apps`.
4. Click on `Install Custom App`.
5. Click on `YAML`.
6. Replace the contents with the `cooloer-control.yaml` file.
7. Update your `source` to match your external drive. By default it will save in `ZimaOS-HD/AppData/coolercontrol`
